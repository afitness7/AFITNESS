START WITH A PLAN

Simple workout plans

Choose a beginner-friendly routine. Start light, warm up first and rest when needed.

🏠 Beginner full body

3 days/week · Bodyweight

  • Bodyweight squats · 3 × 10
  • Wall or knee push-ups · 3 × 8
  • Glute bridges · 3 × 12
  • Plank · 3 × 20 seconds

💪 Basic strength

3 days/week · Gym or home

  • Squats · 3 × 10
  • Push-ups or chest press · 3 × 8
  • Rows · 3 × 10
  • Dead bug · 3 × 10

🏃 Cardio starter

3–5 days/week · Low impact option

  • Easy walk · 5 minutes
  • Brisk walk · 10–15 minutes
  • Slow walk cooldown · 5 minutes
  • Gentle stretches
